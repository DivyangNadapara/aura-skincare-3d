AHA / BHA + Retinoids

Both increase cellular turnover through distinct pathways. Concurrent application overwhelms the stratum corneum, causing acute barrier disruption, burning, erythema, and increased transepidermal water loss (TEWL).

Dermatological Protocol:

Alternate nights (e.g. BHA on Monday/Thursday, Retinoid on Tuesday/Friday/Sunday). Always follow with a ceramide cream.

Acid Equilibrium Kinetics

pH & Free Acid Bioavailability Reactor

For an exfoliating acid to work, it must exist in its un-ionized free acid state. Calculate free acid bioavailability using the Henderson-Hasselbalch equation: α = 1 / (1 + 10^(pH - pKa)).

Optimal Free Acid Equilibrium (pH 3.6 · pKa 3.6)

At pH 3.6, exactly 50% of Glycolic Acid molecules exist as free lipophilic acid capable of traversing the stratum corneum, while 50% is buffered to minimize caustic chemical burns. If pH rises to 5.5, free acid plummets to under 1.2%, rendering the chemical peel completely ineffective.
